Layers of TransformerEncoderLayer

input: (seq_len x d_model)

First, goes through multiheaded attention:
W_q, W_k, W_v; b_q, b_k, b_v for input projection

Then, output of attention gets multiplied by output projection matrix:
W_o, b_o

Then, original input gets added back as a residual connection

Then, the layer gets normalized
norm1.weight, norm1.bias (Gamma1, Beta1)

Then, goes through two feedforward layers, getting projected into dim_feedformward then back down
linear1.weight (dim_feedforward, d_model); linear1.bias (dim_feedforward)
Activation
linear2.weight (d_model, dim_feedforward); linear2.bias (dim_feedformward)

Another residual connection (add X', the output of the first LayerNorm)

Then, second layer norm
norm2.weight, norm2.bias (Gamma2, Beta2)
