## What recovered the missing rows

This is a direct residual/kink recovery fallback, not a claim that the original committee training now converges to all 120.

1. Use the persistent fp64 recovered prefix and known C5 rows. Obtain output Jacobians by finite-difference queries, map through the known prefix Jacobian, and project off the span of known C5 rows. Its right singular vectors direct image-space exploration via inversion through the recovered prefix.
2. Scan lines between random images and those reachable exploratory endpoints. Exclude kinks explained by the known prefix and known C5 hyperplanes. For the remaining kinks, recover the rank-one output-gradient jump and map its normal back through the prefix.
3. These initial directions are only approximate (typically coefficient errors around `1e-4`). Do **not** count them as recovered. Select candidates using their component outside the known-row span; the current heuristic threshold is 0.4. Track their output-kink surfaces from the discovered anchors with the existing `kink_solve.track_layer`, collect 1,200 points, and fit the augmented hyperplane by robust null-space fitting. Require the native numerical/held-out quality checks and at least 30/32 independent output-kink checks.
4. Add verified **unsigned** hyperplanes to a search-only bank, reducing the residual span without pretending their signs are known. Three rounds recover 9, then 5, then 3 missing hyperplanes: 103 -> 112 -> 117 -> 120.
5. Once all 120 hyperplanes are available, form an input direction dual to each new row through the known prefix: that direction moves the chosen C5 preactivation while holding the other 119 fixed. Query output slopes on both sides. For leaky slope 0.01, the slope-norm ratio is 100 or 0.01. These ratios determine all 17 signs using **68 output queries**. The other C5 directional derivatives are below about `1e-16` in the solved local models. See [`c5_orientation_20261021.log`](c5_orientation_20261021.log).

Unknown teacher C5/F6/head coefficients do not enter candidate search, selection, fitting, validation, or sign decisions. Teacher coefficients are read only by explicitly labelled post-decision scoring. Neither exact F6 nor an exact head is supplied to the recovery algorithm.

The oracle contract is the existing refiner's: **fp64 forward evaluations of the saved teacher**. This is not a claim of the same coefficient precision against an oracle restricted to rounded fp32 responses.

## F6 and head completion

The same direct frontier search, now using the recovered signed C5 prefix, found 69 initial F6 candidates. Surface fitting, fresh kink checks, precision retries, and subsequent residual searches established 83 distinct verified F6 hyperplanes. The C5-specific outside-known-span threshold was removed for this last hidden layer: there is no deeper hidden layer generating foreign kinks, and several real F6 rows have only a small component outside the known-row span.

The last F6 hyperplane did not appear in the ordinary residual/origin/extreme searches. [`recover_last_f6_affine.py`](recover_last_f6_affine.py) recovered its full search direction using **output queries only**:

- At each of the 83 known F6 surfaces, an output slope jump determines its output-column amplitude, independently of the surface's unknown orientation.
- Since `phi(-z) - phi(z) = -1.01 z`, choosing a wrong orientation for a known leaky-ReLU unit contributes an affine term. Fit the affine remainder on 4,096 random inputs. Projecting its input coefficients off the known rows isolates a rank-one unknown output direction; projecting outputs orthogonally to that direction identifies the known orientation bits. Their maximum deviation from 0/1 was `1.99e-8`.
- After subtracting the oriented known units, the remaining affine gradient supplies the **full** last-row direction, rather than only its projection off the known span. Its affine fit maximum error was `6.62e-9`. Prefix extrema along that estimated direction produced the last kink on the **first targeted line**. No unknown teacher row or head coefficient was used.

The last fit passed independent kink checks but initially failed the conservative native uncertainty estimate. Batched independent C5 polishing subsequently improved C5's maximum canonical error from `3.10e-10` to `1.65e-11`. All 120 polish fits passed their native quality checks. This polish normalizes C5 rows, so [`canonicalize_polished_prefix_frame.py`](canonicalize_polished_prefix_frame.py) explicitly transfers the F6 bank and candidate input columns into the new positive channel gauge. The first attempt omitted that transfer and was rejected for insufficient tracked points; it is not the final result.

Against the precise, consistent prefix, the final F6 fit passed the **unchanged** native gate: uncertainty `1.830e-9` versus the `1e-7` limit, held-out validation 1.0, plus **32/32 fresh kink checks**. Its post-decision canonical coefficient error was `2.673e-10`. The final bank is [`unsigned_f6_complete.pt`](unsigned_f6_complete.pt), still search-only until orientation.

[`orient_frontier_from_outputs.py`](orient_frontier_from_outputs.py) selected all 84 F6 signs from slope ratios using 336 output queries, producing [`record_f6_signed_84_20261021.pt`](record_f6_signed_84_20261021.pt). [`fit_lenet_head.py`](fit_lenet_head.py) then fitted the head from 4,096 labels. [`verify_complete_lenet.py`](verify_complete_lenet.py) independently reloads the completed model and grades parameters and fresh outputs. Unknown teacher coefficients appear only in post-decision scoring, not in any of these recovery or fitting steps.
